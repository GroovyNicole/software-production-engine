## Required review sets

- Initial and quarterly reviews: all Website Engineering Controls 1-150 plus every applicable Professional Software Engineering Control 1-200.
- Pull request or CMS release: every affected website and software control.
- Migration release: controls 126-150 plus all affected architecture, CSS, accessibility, security, privacy, form, and performance controls.
- Public form or integration change: controls 81-110 plus every other affected control.

## Status vocabulary

- **Verified:** Current evidence proves implementation and operation.
- **Partial:** Some requirements are present, but a material gap remains.
- **Missing:** The required protection or process is absent.
- **Unknown:** Evidence is insufficient; do not infer implementation.
- **Not Applicable:** Outside the defined scope, with a written rationale.

## Status vocabulary

- **Verified:** Implemented and supported by current evidence.
- **Partial:** Some requirements are implemented, but a material gap remains.
- **Missing:** Required protection or process is absent.
- **Unknown:** Evidence is insufficient; do not assume implementation.
- **Not Applicable:** Outside the reviewed system's scope, with a written rationale.

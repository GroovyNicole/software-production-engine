# Phase 0 intake - identity, ownership, and boundaries

[Intake index](../project-intake-template.md) | [Next phase](phase-1.md)

These answers are required before discovery can be considered complete.

1. What is the exact project or product name?
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
2. Which company or organization owns the project, its accounts, its data, and its intellectual property?
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
3. Who is the accountable decision-maker, and who may approve scope, spending, production changes, and launch?
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
4. What problem is being solved, for whom, and what measurable outcome would make the project worthwhile?
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
5. What repository, GitHub owner, local folder, work-tracking workspace/team, domains, cloud accounts, and billing accounts are in scope? Record immutable IDs where available.
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
6. Is this a new build, migration, remediation, maintenance effort, or retirement? What already exists?
   - Answer: `UNANSWERED`
7. What actions may assistants perform without asking, what actions require confirmation, and what actions are prohibited?
   - Answer: `Read-only inspection inside the authorized repository is allowed. External writes and consequential actions require explicit authorization unless replaced by a confirmed project-specific policy.`

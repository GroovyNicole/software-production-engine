# Governance and accountability controls 1-10

[Standard index](../website-engineering-standard.md) | [Previous module](../website-engineering-standard.md) | [Next module](controls-011-020-discovery.md)

## Controls

1. The website has a documented purpose, intended audiences, business outcomes, and measurable success criteria.
2. A named business owner is accountable for content, legal approvals, budget, and launch decisions.
3. The project scope distinguishes migration, redesign, new functionality, content work, and deferred work.
4. Roles are explicit for product ownership, design, development, content, accessibility, security, privacy, SEO, and operations.
5. Requirements and acceptance criteria are recorded before implementation and remain traceable to delivered pages and features.
6. The website is risk-classified based on public exposure, administrator access, forms, uploads, payments, sensitive data, and legal claims.
7. Required legal, privacy, accessibility, advertising, and brand reviews are identified without treating engineering guidance as legal advice.
8. External vendors, plugins, themes, fonts, analytics tools, embeds, CDNs, form processors, and email services are inventoried and owned.
9. The repository, work tracker, content ledger, design system, and production CMS each have a documented source-of-truth role.
10. Material changes use documented approval, release, rollback, and evidence-retention procedures.

## Applying this module

Record status, evidence or Not Applicable rationale, risk, owner, tracked work, target date, verification method, and recheck trigger for every applicable control. Store project-specific decisions and evidence locations in the project's `AGENTS.md` and linked state files.
